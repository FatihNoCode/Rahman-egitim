This app bundles the Arabic typefaces [Amiri](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Amiri) (v30) and [Scheherazade New](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Scheherazade+New) (v21), both used under the [SIL Open Font License 1.1](https://openfontlicense.org/). The `arabic` subsets are vendored in `src/assets/fonts/` and declared in `src/styles/fonts.css`.

The Elif-Ba word levels stream word-by-word Quran recitation audio from the [Quran.com / Quran Foundation](https://quran.com/developers) public CDN (`audio.qurancdn.com`), with word positions taken from the [Quran Foundation Content API](https://api-docs.quran.foundation/). Used under their [developer terms](https://api-docs.quran.foundation/legal/developer-terms/); audio is streamed, not bundled or cached.
